What to turn on in Stripe

Create a Stripe secret key for the checkout service, then let the service create Checkout Sessions with inline product data, SKU metadata, and the buyer’s idea id. Turn on successful-payment emails in your Stripe customer email settings if you want alerts after every sale.

The site only needs the package mapping and the Stripe secret key. Full Build & Deploy is handled as an inquiry flow, so you can review scope and send a scheduling link before you commit.

Stripe handles the fixed-package checkout. Full Build & Deploy starts with email and a scheduled call. All sales final. No refunds or partial credits.
